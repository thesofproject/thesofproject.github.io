Convert linear complex spectra from FFT into Mel band energies in desired logarithmic format.

Parameters
[in]mel_fbStruct with filterbank parameters and filter coefficients to apply.
[in]fft_outArray of complex numbers from FFT in Q1.15 format.
[out]power_spectraArray of linear power spectra, needed scratch are that is half + 1 side of fft_out. The data can be discarded after if no use.
[out]mel_logArray of Q9.7 log/log10/10log10 format Mel band energies.
[in]bitshiftA shift left scale that has been possibly applied to FFT. This will be subtracted from the log or decibels notation.
