Detailed Description

Voice Activity Detection based on Mel spectrum energy.

This VAD operates on the Q9.23 Mel log spectrum values produced by the MFCC component. It tracks a per-bin noise floor that follows the signal downward instantly and rises slowly, then computes a speech-weighted energy delta above the floor.
