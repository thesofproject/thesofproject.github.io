33/* Each channel map specifies which channel from input (buffer between host
34 * and smart amp - source_chan_map[] or feedback buffer between smart amp and
35 * demux - feedback_chan_map[]) will be copied to specific smart amp output
36 * channel. Value -1 means that for this output channel we will not take any
37 * channel from specific input.
38 *
39 * E.g. assuming that a smart amplifier input stream is configured
40 * with channels parameter set to 2, feedback stream with channels set to 8
41 * and smart amplifier output stream with channels set to 4 (smart amplifier
42 * converts stream from 2 to 4 channels) and source/feedback_ch_map's are as
43 * follows:
44 *
45 * source_ch_map = [0, 1, -1, -1, -1 ,-1 ,-1, -1]
46 * feedback_ch_map = [-1, -1, 0, 1, -1, -1, -1, -1]
47 *
48 * As a result smart amplifier test component will procces source and feedback
49 * streams in following way:
50 *
51 *
52 *
53 * PLAYBACK
54 * STREAM +---+
55 * | 0 +------------+
56 * +---+ | SMART AMPLIFIER
57 * | 1 +---------+ | OUTPUT
58 * +---+ | | +---+
59 * | +-->+ 0 |
60 * | +---+
61 * +----->+ 1 |
62 * +---+
63 * +---+ +--->+ 2 |
64 * FEEDBACK | 0 +-----------+ +---+
65 * STREAM +---+ +->| 3 |
66 * | 1 +-------------+ +---+
67 * +---+ | 4 |
68 * | 2 | +---+
69 * +---+ | 5 |
70 * | 3 | +---+
71 * +---+ | 6 |
72 * | 4 | +---+
73 * +---+ | 7 |
74 * | 5 | +---+
75 * +---+
76 * | 6 |
77 * +---+
78 * | 7 |
79 * +---+
80 *
81 */
